*ilo COER li toki e ni tan ona sama taso; jan li pali ala e ilo ona lon tenpo ni. nimi nanpa lon lipu nimi: `JFA/jfa-conformance-suite.py`.*

**selo:** ilo COER li **ilo JFA. selo ona pi esun en sona li ante** — ona li lipu awen pi pona tawa jan ale, li toki e ike kama tawa jan (alerting), li esun ala pi mani pana (credit marketplace). selo li ken ante; supa anpa (floors) li ken ala ante: kipisi ilo pi jan ona sama (sovereign substrate), kipisi sona pi jan lukin (witnessed record), en kipisi pi toki awen (covenant) li lawa sama tawa ijo ale. ilo COER li kama pali la ona li kama jo e ni tan nasin JFA. lawa ale pi pali ala lon tenpo ni li **ijo pi tenpo lili, li jo e nimi, li lon nasin pi wile awen**; ona li weka ala tan lawa lon tenpo ale (o lukin e OPEN-QUESTIONS #1 tawa nasin pi nanpa tenpo):
